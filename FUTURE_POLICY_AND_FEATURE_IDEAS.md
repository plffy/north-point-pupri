## 2. Candidate Fee Policies & Concessions (Under Consideration for Offline Counseling)

These policies were conceived as potential offerings and are retained here for administrative review and counseling desk guidelines:

### A. Sibling Concession
- **Concept:** Tuition fee discount for the second and subsequent child from the same immediate family enrolled concurrently.
- **Proposed Slabs:**
  - Elder Child: Standard fee.
  - Younger Sibling: 10% to 15% concession on tuition fees.
- **Verification Requirement:** Family ration card / Aadhaar linkage verifying parents' names.

### B. Flexible Installment Schedules
- **Concept:** Customized payment schedules accommodating rural, agricultural, or seasonal harvest cash flows in Sitamarhi district.
- **Proposed Options:**
  - Quarterly installments (April, July, October, January).
  - Bi-annual harvest-aligned schedules.
  - Monthly payment without punitive late fees for enrolled rural families.

### C. SCAT (Scholarship-Cum-Admission Test) Slabs
- **Concept:** Performance-based tuition waiver awarded to high-scoring students in the periodic diagnostic exam.
- **Proposed Slabs:**
  - Rank 1–5: Up to 100% waiver on coaching / tuition component.
  - Rank 6–15: 50% waiver.
  - Rank 16–30: 25% waiver.

### D. Special Category Assistance
- Merit awards for single-girl-child families.
- Need-based concessions for children of martyred armed personnel or police officers.
